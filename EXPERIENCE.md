## FANUC Robot Setup — 4 Years

### Scope
Hands-on setup, configuration, and integration of FANUC industrial robots in automotive manufacturing environments.

### Operational Knowledge
- Robot programming basics: TP (Teach Pendant) programming, position registers, I/O configuration
- Integration with PLC systems (typically Allen-Bradley/Rockwell) and safety circuits (E-stops, light curtains)
- Understanding of production cell layout, cycle time optimization, and changeover procedures
- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures — firsthand familiarity with the exact compliance domain of OSHA LOTO Pro

### SCM Relevance
- **Cycle time = throughput.** FANUC robots run at programmed cycle times — any deviation (fault, mis-pick, upstream starvation) ripples downstream. Direct experience with Theory of Constraints in a physical production context.
- **Changeover = setup cost.** Robot retooling and reprogramming between part variants = the setup cost (S) in the EOQ formula.
- **LOTO experience** is the operational credibility behind the OSHA LOTO Pro product.

## eBay Reselling — Micro-Logistics Operations

### Model
Sourcing (thrift stores, liquidation, estate sales) → inspection/cleaning → listing → packing → shipping → returns management.

### Operational Details
- SKU management across 50–200+ active listings at varying points
- Dynamic pricing decisions based on sold comps, condition grading, and fee impact
- Carrier selection: USPS Ground Advantage vs. Priority vs. UPS vs. FedEx — cost/speed tradeoffs per package
- Returns: condition disputes, damage claims, restocking decisions
- Tools: eBay Seller Hub, manual fee calculation (replaced by eBay Tracker project)

### SCM Relevance
This is a complete single-operator supply chain:
- **Sourcing = procurement.** Evaluating lot purchases from liquidation pallets = vendor evaluation + cost-per-unit analysis.
- **Listing cadence = demand signaling.** How quickly items move at what price = real-time demand sensing with zero forecasting infrastructure.
- **Packing/shipping = fulfillment operations.** Carrier selection, packaging cost, dimensional weight pricing — direct last-mile logistics.
- **Returns = reverse logistics.** Condition triage, restocking vs. discard decisions, dispute resolution.
- **No visibility into net profit** was the bleeding neck that produced the eBay Tracker product.
